# Free spreadsheet templates for invoices, expenses and job costs

Eight working Excel templates (`.xlsx`) with live formulas. Free to download, use and change — no account, no email address. They open in Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers.

Each one is explained in full on its own page, including why it is built the way it is.

## Using them

1. Click a template above, then **Download raw file**.
2. Delete the example row and enter your own.
3. In Google Sheets or Numbers, set your locale before importing so dates and £ are read as dates and numbers.

Formulas are filled down to row 500. To go further, select the last formula row and drag it down.

## What they do not do

They record and total. They do not give tax advice, file anything with HMRC, or decide what is allowable — use HMRC's guidance or an accountant for that.

## Filling them in without typing

The templates are the easy half. The slow part is turning a folder of supplier invoices — PDFs, scans, phone photos — into rows. [Invoices to Spreadsheets](https://invoicestospreadsheets.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=templates) does that: it reads a batch of invoices into an editable table with every line item, shows each value beside the original so you can check it, and exports to Excel or CSV. You can [try one invoice free without an account](https://invoicestospreadsheets.com/tools/invoice-to-excel?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=templates).
